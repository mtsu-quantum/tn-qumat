### Overview {#overview .scroll-mt-32}

![AMPLIFY Scholars 2026](/images/news/amplify-2026/amp_title.jpg)

The **AMPLIFY Scholar Program - Quantum Research Track** is a paid, 10-week summer research experience for undergraduate STEM students. It is part of the TN-QuMat-ORNL consortium, a DOE RENEW initiative bringing together Middle Tennessee State University (MTSU), Tennessee State University (TSU), Fisk University, Meharry Medical College, and Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL) to build a quantum-ready workforce in Tennessee.

**Applications for Summer 2026 are closed.** From about 70 applicants, 12 scholars were selected. [Read about the 2026 AMPLIFY Scholars](/news/amplify-2026/).

### What Scholars Receive

- **Paid research stipend:** 10 weeks at $17.50 per hour, about $3,500 in total
- **Faculty-mentored research** in quantum materials, using ORNL's world-class computing and TSU experimental facilities
- **Scientific reading:** paper reading workshops and primary literature training
- **Presenting research:** poster preparation and presentation support for Scholars Week and national conferences
- **Graduate school coaching:** personal statements, program selection, and interview preparation
- **Fellowship guidance:** NSF REU, DOE SULI at ORNL, NSF GRFP, and the Goldwater Scholarship
- **Cohort community:** regular professional development sessions and cohort meetings

### Research Tracks {#topics .scroll-mt-32}

**A. Modeling and Simulations of Quantum Systems.** Theoretical and computational modeling of strongly correlated electrons, disorder effects, and quantum phase transitions.

**B. First-Principles Simulations of Materials.** Density functional theory and ab initio methods applied to topological insulators, Weyl semimetals, and quantum information-relevant materials.

**C. Synthesis and Characterization of Low-Dimensional Quantum Structures; Quantum Optics.** Laboratory synthesis, thin-film growth, and spectroscopy of 2D materials and novel superconductors, and quantum optics.

### Who Should Apply

- Any undergraduate STEM student, including Physics, Chemistry, Computer Science, Mathematics, Biology, Engineering, Data Science, and more
- Any year, freshman through senior, with no prior quantum research required
- Students enrolled at MTSU, TSU, Fisk University, or Meharry Medical College
